01

🎯 Problem

Finding relevant senior tech jobs in India means manually checking Naukri, Cutshort, Wellfound, iimjobs, Greenhouse boards, company career pages — every day. Each site has different filters, different freshness, and 90% noise. You spend 2–3 hours a week just finding jobs worth reading, before you even evaluate fit.

Most job boards show you what's popular, not what matches your specific profile — your stack, your seniority, your salary floor, your preference for product companies over service firms.

RoleMiner automates the entire discovery layer. You provide your resume and preferences once. Every run returns a shortlist of scored, ranked roles — with an explanation of why each fits or doesn't.

02

⚡ How It Works

1

You provide resume + preferences

Drop your resume PDF and fill a simple YAML file with what you're looking for — domains, stack, location, salary floor, company type. One-time setup.

2

RoleMiner discovers relevant companies

First run builds a company registry — finds companies matching your preferences across all sources. This runs once and updates incrementally.

3

Fresh jobs fetched from all sources

Every run pulls jobs posted in the last 7 days from 8+ sources in parallel — ATS APIs, job portals, career pages. No stale listings.

4

AI scores each job against your profile

An LLM reads your resume and each job description, scores fit 0–10, identifies skill matches and gaps, and writes a one-line reason.

5

Dashboard shows ranked shortlist

Open the web app — see scored job cards, filter by location/score/company type, search semantically, compare jobs, view skill gap breakdown per role.

⏱️ Total time per run: ~3–5 minutes. Time you save: 2–3 hours/week of manual searching.
03

📥 What You Provide

Two files. That's all RoleMiner needs to start.

📄 Input 1 — Your Resume PDF

Drop your existing resume PDF. RoleMiner extracts your skills, experience, education, and contact details automatically. No reformatting needed — it works with any resume layout.

Extracted fields used for scoring: current title · years of experience · tech stack · companies worked at · education · location

⚙️ Input 2 — Search Preferences YAML file

Fill this once. Edit anytime to change what you're looking for. 

# search_profile.yaml — edit this to define your job search

# What kind of roles
domains:
  - frontend
  - fullstack

keywords:                    # job titles or role types you want
  - "senior frontend engineer"
  - "founding engineer"
  - "staff engineer"

# Your tech stack
stack:
  - React
  - TypeScript
  - Node.js

# Location
locations:
  - Bangalore
  - Remote
  - Hyderabad
work_mode:                   # remote | hybrid | onsite
  - remote
  - hybrid

# Salary
salary_min_lpa: 30           # ₹ Lakhs Per Annum — filters out below-threshold roles

# Company preferences
company_types:
  - product                  # product | startup | enterprise
  - startup
exclude_service_companies: true   # filters TCS/Infosys/Wipro/Accenture type
preferred_funding_stages:  # leave empty to include all
  - Series B
  - Series C
  - Series D
  - Public

# Your situation
notice_period_days: 30        # 0 | 30 | 60 | 90 — affects urgency scoring
esop_preferred: true          # flags roles that mention equity/ESOP

# Explicitly block these companies
exclude_companies:
  - TCS
  - Infosys
  - Wipro
  - Cognizant
  - HCL
ℹ️ Nothing else needed. RoleMiner reads these two files and does the rest — company discovery, scraping, scoring, ranking.

07

🇮🇳 India-Specific

Product vs Service Filter

RoleMiner classifies every company as product or service. Set exclude_service_companies: true to never see TCS/Infosys/Wipro/Cognizant-type roles. This is automatic — you don't have to maintain a blocklist.

LPA Salary Handling

All salaries normalised to ₹ LPA. Set your floor once in the profile. Jobs below threshold are filtered before scoring — you never see them.

Notice Period Awareness

Declare your notice period (0/30/60/90 days). Jobs that say "immediate joiner only" get flagged when your notice doesn't match. Score is adjusted accordingly.

ESOP Detection

RoleMiner detects ESOP / equity mentions in JDs and flags them. If esop_preferred: true, these rank higher in scoring.

Funding Stage Signal

Company funding stage (Seed / Series A–D / Public) shown on every card. Filter by stage. Prefer Series B+ for stability, Seed for outsized upside.

Work Mode Clarity

"Remote" in India often means hybrid. RoleMiner extracts actual work mode from JD text — WFH / WFO / hybrid — not just what the job title says.

🚫 Out of Scope

  • ✗ Submitting applications — RoleMiner finds and scores jobs. Applying is a separate step (handled by JobPilot apply agent).
  • ✗ LinkedIn scraping — ban risk too high. Excluded entirely.
  • ✗ Resume generation — out of scope for this tool (handled by JobPilot resume agent).
  • ✗ Recruiter outreach — RoleMiner surfaces jobs, not recruiters or hiring managers.
  • ✗ Global jobs — India-first. Remote roles included but non-India international roles deprioritised.
